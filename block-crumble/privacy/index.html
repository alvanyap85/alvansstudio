Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Block Crumble ("the game") is made by Alvan's Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we don't run any servers and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and the optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your phone only:

  • settings: sound, music, block style, difficulty and effects;
  • progress: stages reached, stars and best scores, your Daily Dig streak and your helpers;
  • the name you type for the weekly scoreboard, and the scores on that scoreboard;
  • whether you own "Remove ads".

We never receive this information. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some games, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch to continue a game. There are no banner ads.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most Android phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The game asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, so it can restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Because we don't collect or store your personal information on our own systems, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Information collected by Google is handled under Google's own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how information is used, such as online leaderboards, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

Contact
